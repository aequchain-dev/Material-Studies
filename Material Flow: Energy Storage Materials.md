## §I — GOVERNING THESIS

The corpus **exits the battery-materials race** (cobalt, NMC, lithium geopolitics) on three axes simultaneously:
1. **Chemistry substitution** — every storage chemistry is chosen for safety, longevity, sustainability — *never* energy density: *"LiFePO₄ chosen over NMC for safety, longevity, sustainability… energy density lower than NMC (safety trade-off)"* (aequmoto, stated twice in its own optimization log).
2. **Layered architecture** — harvest + buffer + betavoltaic baseline means no single cell carries the load (AEQUIGEN's six-mode design).
3. **The EFE context** — energy itself is free (renewable), so storage optimizes for *cycling economics and material circularity*, not gravimetric extremes. The battery periodic table is treated as **choice architecture**: LFP → Na-ion → V-flow → iron-air → zinc-air, each eliminating a critical metal (Co, La, Li-geopolitics, Li, Ag).
