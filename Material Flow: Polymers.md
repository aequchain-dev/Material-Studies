## §I — GOVERNING THESIS

Polymers are the one material class the corpus treats as **fundamentally transient**: grown from plants, used, then returned — *"bio-degrade or recycle"* is the explicit end-state rule of the aequcity polymer stream. The doctrine: **bio-polymers cycle through biology; legacy plastics cycle through industry; petro-plastics are designed out entirely.** No polymer is permitted a landfill terminus: even PVC — "which has few recycling solutions" — is sequestered into evermateria eco-bricks whose own waste "can be recycled back into bricks… zero waste from start to finish."

**The three polymer classes:**
1. **Bio-polymers** (PLA, PHA, bio-PE) — plant starch/sugar → fermentation → polymer → industrial composting → CO₂ → biomass → repeat *(the biological cycle)*
2. **Recycled legacy plastics** (HDPE, PET, PVC) — urban-mined waste → eco-bricks / re-extrusion *(the industrial cycle)*
3. **Petro-polymers** — `[DESIGNED OUT]` replaced by XEROSIL-MATRIX, bio-epoxies, bio-polyols, lignin resins

## §II — ANCHOR LOOP: The Bio-Polymer Cycle `[BLUEPRINT]`

**Sourcing:** D3 bio-production (aequcity: "Bio-production (D3) + recycled (E4)") — corn/sugarcane starch for PLA (Manufactory M1.1: "Corn/sugarcane starch, rigid, 60 °C max"); fermented plant waste for PLA/PHA blend (UMAGCONNEC housing); sugarcane bagasse as the SA-optimized universal bio-feedstock.
**Propagation:** Manufactory polymer toolheads — A1 FDM dual-nozzle 300 °C (PLA, PHA, Bio-PE, rPET) · A3 pellet extruder 350 °C (pelletized bio-plastics, large-format) · A6 bio-composite continuous-fiber 250 °C (hemp/flax + bio-resin) · D4 friction-stir welding (polymers) · E5 heat-gun thermal processing.
**End-of-life:** industrial composting (UMAGCONNEC: ASTM D6400, 90 days to nutrient soil) → home composting (PHA gaskets, V3 iteration "faster-composting") → E2 polymer recycling (aequcity: "Bio-based chemicals, polymer recycling, contained atmosphere, catalytic converters") → carbon cycle.

**Secondary anchor — the PVC sequestration loop (evermateria):** plastic waste (PVC etc., concrete waste, glass waste) **paid to take** → eco-bricks/pavers **FREE to build** → brick waste → recycled back into bricks. *The unrecyclable becomes permanent useful mass.*

## §IV — THE COMPLETE POLYMER INVENTORY

**Bio-production (D3, compostable):** PLA (M1.1; 60 °C limit) · PHA (PHA/PLA blends; PHA fast-compost gaskets) · bio-PE (chemically PE — **recyclable, NOT biodegradable** `[flag]`) · cellulose acetate (aequcity stream) · lignin resin (aequvivum: 35–40% of DLGC — the structural bio-resin) · bio-epoxy (flax composite) · bio-polyol PU (linseed) · alginate/algae biopolymer (UMAGCONNEC insulation with mycelium) · spider-silk protein (UMAGCONNEC pin guides, with bamboo fiber) · bio-binders (asphalt, biochar catalyst supports, M5.1 conductive bio-ink, M4.5 bio-calcium).

**Recycled-input polymers (E2/E4):** rPET · rHDPE · PVC (eco-brick sequestration) · crumb rubber (waste tires → rubberized bio-asphalt) · bio-PVC.

**Composites (polymer matrix):** Hemp-PLA (M3.1) · Flax-Bio-Epoxy (M3.2) · Bamboo-Bio-Resin (M3.3) · Cork Composite (M3.6, shock-absorbing) · mycelium composites (EMI shielding, sensor substrates, insulation, packaging) · rubber-graphene damping (aequmoto mounts).

**Conductive polymers:** PEDOT:PSS (ITO substitute) · M5.1 conductive bio-ink (carbon + bio-binder → printed circuits/sensors) · 3deconomy conductive silver paste + hydrogel bio-ink.

## §IX — CONCLUSION

The polymer doctrine: **biology makes it, industry re-melts it, nothing buries it.** Compostable bio-polymers close through the carbon cycle; recycled plastics close through E2/E4; the chemically-stubborn (PVC) are sequestered into permanent bricks; and the petro-plastic itself is replaced by a self-healing, fungal-triggered, invasive-plant-derived nanocomposite that is stronger than polypropylene. *The corpus's unique move is treating "unrecyclable" as a design brief, not a dead end.*
