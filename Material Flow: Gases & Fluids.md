## §I — GOVERNING THESIS

Gases are the corpus's **continuously-cycled working substances — never consumed, only separated, reacted, and recaptured.** The aequcity stream rule is the cleanest single line in the entire material program:

> **"Gases: O₂, N₂, H₂, CO₂, Ar — Air separation (E2) + electrolysis — Cycled continuously."**

The category has one structural feature no other class has: **CO₂ is simultaneously the waste product of the old economy and the feedstock of the apex material** (diamond/graphene — see Carbon study). The gas program is therefore organized around a single inversion: *the atmosphere is a mine with an infinite, self-refilling seam.*

## §II — ANCHOR LOOP: The Air-Separation + Electrolysis + CO₂-Capture Cycle `[BLUEPRINT]`

**Sourcing:** E2 air separation splits the atmosphere into **O₂, N₂, Ar**; water electrolysis (renewable-powered) yields **H₂ + O₂**; **DAC** (CIL: 6,200 t CO₂/yr per facility; 10,000 target; 8,400 verified) mines the atmosphere; biological capture (phyto-sterile agriculture, G-zone forest) pumps additional CO₂ into biomass.
**Propagation:** H₂ → EAF steel + high-temp processes + fuel cells + **surplus H₂ sales** · N₂ → **Fe₁₆N₂ magnet synthesis ("recycled iron + nitrogen gas")** + inert atmospheres (E2 contained atmosphere) · CH₄/biogas → **CVD graphene feedstock** + energy · CO₂ → CVD diamond growth + Ferrock carbonation + greenhouse enrichment + credits.
**Recapture:** *"ENHANCED growth → more CO₂ capture"* — the greenhouse loop (aequcity atmosphere-CO₂ section); **DAC_CAPTURE + BIOCHAR_STORAGE** on-chain credits verify every tonne; waste gases → E1 closed-loop filtration, zero-emission target.
**The loop closes at the sky:** carbon out of the air, into permanent materials and soil, forever.

## §IV — INDUSTRIAL METABOLISM

- **E2 Chemical Processing** (aequcity): "Bio-based chemicals, polymer recycling, contained atmosphere, catalytic converters" — the gas-handling heart: air separation, electrolysis, digestion, controlled atmospheres.
- **CIL**: the CO₂ consumer-in-chief — DAC units + carbonization reactors + 60× CVD chambers; every operation *"captures more CO₂"* than it emits (net-negative enterprise).
