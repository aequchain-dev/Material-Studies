## §I — THE GOVERNING THESIS

Two sentences govern every metal decision in the ecosystem:

> **1. The Elements Thesis** (Earth-circularity theorem, metals corollary — validated): *"Elements do not degrade. Iron, steel, aluminum, copper, gold, silver, rare earths, platinum group — all metals are elements. They may disperse, but dispersion is an engineering problem, not a physical barrier. > **2. The Substitution-First Doctrine:** eliminate demand for critical metals *before* building recycling supply for them. 65% of osmium use is substitutable; every REE and PGM has a complete substitution table; the newest designs *reject* NdFeB outright rather than recycle it.
